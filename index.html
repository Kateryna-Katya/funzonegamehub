Audio Game Hub

Your Fun Zone of Sound-Powered Games

Audio Game Hub is a collection of 12+ quick arcade mini-games you play with your ears. Disarm bombs, shoot arrows, escape labyrinths and beat your friends — all guided by sound. Sighted or not, everyone plays on the same level.

The Games

Pick Your Game. Trust Your Ears.

Every mini-game in the hub has its own sound world. Some test your memory, some your reflexes, some your nerve. Here are the crowd favourites:

  • Bomb Disarmer

    Listen for the ticking, find the right wire, and keep your hands steady. One wrong cut and it's game over.

  • Super Simon

    The classic memory game, rebuilt for your ears. Repeat the growing sequence of tones for as long as you can.

  • Archery

    Draw, aim by sound, release. The closer the tone, the closer you are to the bullseye.

  • Labyrinth

    Navigate winding corridors using audio cues alone. Echoes tell you where the walls are — and where the exit hides.

  • Animal Farm Memory

    Match pairs of animal sounds scattered across the board. Simple to learn, surprisingly tricky to master.

  • Blocks

    Stack falling pieces guided by pitch and rhythm. The higher the tower, the faster it gets.

...and six more games waiting inside the hub. New favourites are added over time.

Why Sound

Games You Don't Have to Look At

Most mobile games fight for your eyes. Audio Game Hub does the opposite — it hands the whole experience to your ears, and that changes everything.

  • Point 1

    Play with your eyes closed

    Every game is fully playable without looking at the screen. Lean back, close your eyes, and let 3D audio guide you through each round.

  • Point 2

    Built for everyone

    Audio Game Hub was designed from day one to be equally playable for sighted, low-vision and blind players. No separate "accessible mode" — the same game, the same rules, the same fun for all.

  • Point 3

    Party modes for up to 10 players

    Pass-the-phone tournaments turn any living room into an arcade. Up to 10 players compete in the same session, and nobody gets an unfair advantage by watching the screen.

  • Point 4

    Train while you play

    Sound-based games are a genuine workout for your brain. Regular rounds sharpen auditory memory, reaction speed and focus — without ever feeling like an exercise.

How to Start

Three Steps to the Fun Zone

  • Step 1

    Download

    Grab Audio Game Hub free from Google Play. It's a small download and runs on most Android phones and tablets.

  • Step 2

    Put your headphones on

    Stereo headphones make the 3D audio shine. You'll hear exactly where the target, the wall, or the ticking bomb is.

  • Step 1

    Play

    Start with Animal Farm Memory if you want an easy warm-up, or jump straight into Bomb Disarmer if you like pressure. Invite friends whenever you're ready for party mode.
