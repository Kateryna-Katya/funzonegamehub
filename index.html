Audio Game Hub

Your Fun Zone of Sound-Powered Games

Audio Game Hub is a collection of 12+ quick arcade mini-games you play with your ears. Disarm bombs, shoot arrows, escape labyrinths and beat your friends — all guided by sound. Sighted or not, everyone plays on the same level.

The Games

Pick Your Game. Trust Your Ears.

Every mini-game in the hub has its own sound world. Some test your memory, some your reflexes, some your nerve. Here are the crowd favourites:

  • Bomb Disarmer

    Listen for the ticking, find the right wire, and keep your hands steady. One wrong cut and it's game over.

  • Super Simon

    The classic memory game, rebuilt for your ears. Repeat the growing sequence of tones for as long as you can.

  • Archery

    Draw, aim by sound, release. The closer the tone, the closer you are to the bullseye.

  • Labyrinth

    Navigate winding corridors using audio cues alone. Echoes tell you where the walls are — and where the exit hides.

  • Animal Farm Memory

    Match pairs of animal sounds scattered across the board. Simple to learn, surprisingly tricky to master.

  • Blocks

    Stack falling pieces guided by pitch and rhythm. The higher the tower, the faster it gets.

...and six more games waiting inside the hub. New favourites are added over time.
